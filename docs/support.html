🚀 Getting Started

Q: How do I add my first income or expense?

Navigate to the Income or Expenses tab at the bottom of the screen. Tap any category card and enter your amount. You can add daily, monthly, or yearly amounts for each category.

Your dashboard will update instantly with your live financial meter!

Q: What do the different frequencies mean (daily, monthly, yearly)?

Daily: Recurring daily expenses/income (e.g., daily coffee: $5/day)

Monthly: Monthly bills or income (e.g., rent: $1,200/month)

Yearly: Annual expenses/income (e.g., insurance: $1,200/year)

BudgetMeter automatically converts and combines all frequencies to show you accurate totals.

Q: How does the live meter work?

The live meter shows your financial flow accumulating in real-time, second-by-second. It calculates your net flow (income minus expenses) and displays how much you're earning or spending since you opened the app.

It's like watching your financial life in action! 📈

🔒 Data & Privacy

Q: Where is my financial data stored?

All data is stored locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or have access to any of your financial information.

If you enable iCloud sync in Settings, data is backed up to your private iCloud account. We still cannot access this data—it's encrypted and only accessible to you.

Q: How do I enable iCloud sync?

Currently, iCloud sync is automatic if you're signed into iCloud on your device.

Your data syncs across all devices using the same Apple ID.

To disable sync, you would need to turn off iCloud for BudgetMeter in iOS Settings → Your Name → iCloud.

Q: How do I export my data?

Premium Feature: Go to Settings → Data & Privacy → Export Data

Choose from three formats:

  • PDF: Beautiful formatted report
  • CSV: Spreadsheet-compatible format
  • JSON: Complete data export for developers
Q: How do I delete all my data?

Go to Settings → Data & Privacy → Reset All Data

⚠️ This action is permanent and cannot be undone. All income, expense, goal, and bill data will be deleted.

🔧 Troubleshooting

Q: My calculations don't look right. What should I check?

Verify these settings:

  • Check that amounts are entered in the correct frequency (daily/monthly/yearly)
  • Ensure your currency is set correctly in Settings → Currency
  • Review all categories to make sure amounts are accurate

BudgetMeter uses precise calculations with astronomically accurate constants (30.4375 days/month, 365.25 days/year).

Q: Face ID/Touch ID isn't working

Check these steps:

  1. Ensure biometric lock is enabled in Settings → Security
  2. Verify Face ID/Touch ID is set up in iOS Settings
  3. Make sure BudgetMeter has permission to use biometrics
  4. Try disabling and re-enabling the feature

If issues persist, restart your device.

Q: The app crashed or is acting strange

Try these solutions:

  1. Force quit and reopen: Swipe up from bottom (or double-click Home), swipe BudgetMeter away, reopen
  2. Restart your device: Turn iPhone/iPad off and back on
  3. Update the app: Check App Store for updates
  4. Update iOS: Go to Settings → General → Software Update

If problems continue, contact support (see below).

Q: How do I change the app language?

Go to Settings → Language and select from 10 available languages:

  • English, Turkish, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Japanese, Chinese (Simplified), Arabic

The app will update instantly without restart.

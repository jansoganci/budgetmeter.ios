Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 23, 2025
TL;DR: BudgetMeter collects ZERO personal data. Everything stays on your device. We don't track you, sell data, or use analytics. Your privacy is absolute.

1. Introduction

Welcome to BudgetMeter. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data (spoiler: we don't collect any). Your financial information is yours alone, and we're committed to keeping it that way.

Developer: Umurcan Soganci
Contact: umursoganci@gmail.com

2. Information We Collect

Short answer: None.

BudgetMeter does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal information or financial data. All your budget tracking data remains exclusively on your device.

We do NOT collect:

  • Personal identification (name, email, phone number)
  • Financial data (income, expenses, savings goals)
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Usage analytics or crash reports
  • Contacts, photos, or other device data

3. How We Use Your Information

We don't use your information because we don't have access to it. Your financial data is stored locally on your device using:

  • iOS UserDefaults: For app settings and preferences
  • Core Data: For your financial categories, transactions, and goals
  • Local SQLite database: Encrypted by iOS, never transmitted

4. iCloud Sync (Optional Feature)

If you enable iCloud sync in Settings, your financial data is stored in your private iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit framework.

Important points about iCloud sync:

  • Sync is optional and disabled by default
  • Data is stored in your personal iCloud account, not our servers
  • We cannot access your iCloud data
  • Apple's iCloud Privacy Policy applies to synced data
  • You can disable sync anytime in Settings → Data & Privacy
  • Disabling sync does not delete your data from iCloud; manage this in iOS Settings → Your Name → iCloud

5. Face ID / Touch ID Usage

BudgetMeter uses Face ID, Touch ID, or Optic ID (biometric authentication) to protect your financial data and ensure secure access to the app.

How biometric authentication works:

  • Biometric data is processed entirely by iOS and never leaves your device
  • We never access your biometric data
  • Authentication is optional and can be enabled/disabled in Settings
  • If you disable biometric lock, your data remains on your device

6. Third-Party Services

BudgetMeter does not use any third-party services, including:

  • ❌ Analytics services (Google Analytics, Firebase, etc.)
  • ❌ Advertising networks
  • ❌ Crash reporting tools
  • ❌ Social media integrations
  • ❌ External databases or servers

The only Apple services we use are:

  • ✅ StoreKit: For processing in-app purchases (handled by Apple)
  • ✅ CloudKit (optional): For iCloud sync (your private account only)

7. In-App Purchases

Premium feature purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. We do not collect payment information.

What Apple handles:

  • Payment processing and billing
  • Purchase receipts and verification
  • Refund requests

Apple's Privacy Policy applies to purchase transactions.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have complete control:

  • View all data: Use Settings → Data & Privacy → Export Data (PDF/CSV/JSON)
  • Delete all data: Go to Settings → Data & Privacy → Reset All Data
  • Uninstall app: Deleting BudgetMeter removes all local data immediately

iCloud data: If you enabled iCloud sync, delete synced data from iOS Settings → Your Name → iCloud → Manage Storage → BudgetMeter → Delete Data.

9. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Even though we don't collect data, we respect your privacy rights under GDPR (European Union) and CCPA (California):

GDPR Rights (EU Users)

  • Right to Access: View all your data via Settings → Data Export
  • Right to Rectification: Edit any data directly in the app
  • Right to Erasure: Delete all data via Settings → Reset All Data
  • Right to Data Portability: Export data in PDF, CSV, or JSON format
  • Right to Object: Disable iCloud sync anytime

CCPA Rights (California Users)

  • Right to Know: All data is visible within the app
  • Right to Delete: Use Reset All Data feature
  • Right to Opt-Out of Sale: Not applicable (we don't sell data)

Important: BudgetMeter does not sell, rent, or share your personal information with anyone.

10. International Data Transfers

If you enable iCloud sync, your data may be transferred to Apple's servers, which may be located outside your country. Apple complies with GDPR requirements for international data transfers using Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs).

11. Children's Privacy

BudgetMeter is not directed to children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

12. Security

While we don't collect data, we protect your on-device data through:

  • iOS encryption: All local data is encrypted by iOS
  • Biometric lock: Optional Face ID/Touch ID protection
  • No network transmission: Data never leaves your device (except optional iCloud sync)
  • Secure coding practices: Following Apple's security guidelines
